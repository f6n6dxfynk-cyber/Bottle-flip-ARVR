BOTTLE FLIP IN VR
Desktop project package — 31 October 2026

1. PLAY
Open Demo/BottleFlipVR.app.
Tap and release Space, or click outside the instruction panel.
Wait for Ready between throws. Three landings finish the course.
Press R or Replay to start again. Easy assistance is enabled.

2. PRESENT
Open Presentation/BottleFlipVR_10_Minute_Presentation.pptx in PowerPoint.
Start Slide Show. The 35-second video on slide 7 plays automatically.
Speaker notes contain a ten-minute schedule and speaking prompts.
The technical revision covers launch equations, collider settings, landing checks,
component events, state guards, replay handling and the tested input ranges.
The slides use the charcoal, lime and blue theme from the supplied references.
Helvetica Neue was chosen to match the reference's clean sans-serif lettering.
The exact font cannot be identified reliably from the screenshots alone.
The standalone app is the interactive demo; the embedded video is a recording.
Keep Demo and Presentation together for the live-demo link.
For a longer live attempt, switch to the app after the ten-minute talk.

3. READ
Report/BottleFlipVR_Project_Report.pdf is the 22-page ready-to-read report.
The DOCX beside it is editable. It includes a 20-paper comparison and sources.
Harsh, Rudra and Swayam receive an equal one-third contribution allocation.

4. EDIT IN UNITY
Add/open UnityProject/BottleFlipVR in Unity Hub using Unity 6000.5.7f1.
Hub launches Unity Editor. Then open Assets/Scenes/BottleFlipVR.unity.
Press Play to simulate. Stop Play before editing and saving.
Select Bottle to adjust assistance and timing in the Inspector.
Select platforms to move or resize them. Test the complete course after changes.
Use File > Build Profiles, or Bottle Flip > Build macOS Demo, to rebuild.
The project is complete and editable; it is not a loose script.

VALIDATION

courses, initial readiness and 7 completion/replay/recovery edge checks.
Manual keyboard and mouse play also reached 300 points and replay reset to 0.

BOTTLE FLIP IN VR — UNITY PROJECT

Open this entire folder in Unity 6000.5.7f1.
Open Assets/Scenes/BottleFlipVR.unity, then press Play.

Tap and release Space or click outside the instruction panel.
Wait for Ready before the next throw. Three landings finish the course.
R or Replay starts again.

To edit without writing code: stop Play, select Bottle, and change
Assisted Aim, Easy Landing, Easy Flight Time or Landing Assist Duration
in the Inspector. Keep both assistance switches enabled for easy mode.
Move platforms in the Scene view, save, then test the full course.

The game uses simplified physics and landing assistance. Headset input
is future work. The final report and tests are in the parent package.
Unity recreates its Library folder on first import.
